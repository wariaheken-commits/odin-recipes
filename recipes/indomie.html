INGREDIENTS

  • A pack of Indomie noodles depending on your appetite and the number of people eating
  • 1-2 cups of water
  • 3 tomatoes,chopped or blended
  • 1-2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1-2 onions, chopped
  • Cooking oil
  • 1-2 eggs
  • Yoghurt or heavy cream
  • Soy sauce

    INSTRUCTIONS

  1. To a pot add your oil and heat it
  2. Add the chopped onions and garlic, and sauté until fragrant
  3. While this happens add water to a pot add a pinch of salt and a little oil and add your indomie noodles
  4. Add soy sauce and caramelize the onions
  5. Add the blended tomatoes after the onions are broken down
  6. Add cooking yoghurt or heavy cream,add any additional spices including the satchets included in the indomie pack and let it simmer
  7. Drain your noodles then mix into the sauce
  8. In another pot, add water and bring to a boil before cooking your eggs for 5-7 minutes
  9. Once the eggs are cooked, remove from heat and let them cool before peeling and adding to the dish
  10. Serve everything in a ceramic dish or plate and add fresh herbs or green onions for garnish with the eggs cut into halves
