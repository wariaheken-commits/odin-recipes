Fried Rice

Home

Description

Fried rice is a delicious dish made with cooked rice, vegetables,eggs and seasoning. its easy to prepare and makes a great meal.

Ingredients

  • Minced cloves
  • 4 cups of cold, day-old cooked white rice
  • Onions/scallions
  • Fresh ginger
  • 2 Eggs beaaten
  • Soy Sauce
  • Cooking oil

Instructions

  1. Add oil in a wok or pan and heat it until water droplets bounce of it
  2. Add scallions,ginger and cloves
  3. To a bowl beat the eggs and mix the cold rice with the eggs for the rice to absorb the egg essense
  4. Add the egg-rice mixture to the wok and stir-fry until heated through
  5. Season with soy sauce and serve hot
  6. Enjoy with a cold drink
